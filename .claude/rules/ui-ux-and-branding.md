# UI/UX and Branding Rules

- The supplied company website and logo are the source of truth for brand direction.
- If they are missing or inaccessible, ask for the URL/assets or screenshots; do not finalize a guessed theme.
- Centralize design tokens; do not scatter arbitrary colors or one-off styling.
- Keep interfaces responsive, accessible, keyboard-friendly, and consistent.
- Support loading, empty, error, success, disabled, permission-denied, and stale-data states.
- Use charts only when they communicate real, labeled information.
- Clearly label demo data and simulated integrations.
- Avoid excessive animation and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Preserve logo aspect ratio and clear space.
